Hi, I'm Maharshi. I'm a cyber security learner documenting my journey in public, one lab at a time.

## What you'll find here

- **Writeups:** walkthroughs of TryHackMe, Hack The Box and CTF challenges, with the reasoning behind each step, not just the answers
- **Tool notes:** practical guides to tools like Nmap, Wireshark and Burp Suite
- **Concepts explained simply:** networking, Linux, web security and the fundamentals that everything else builds on
- **Learning roadmap:** what I'm studying, what worked, and what didn't

## Why I write

Writing is the best way I know to turn half-understood ideas into real knowledge. If these notes also save someone else a few hours of confusion, even better.

## Focus areas

- Network and web application security
- Ethical hacking and penetration testing fundamentals
- Defensive security and threat analysis
- CTFs and hands-on labs
